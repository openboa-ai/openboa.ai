# OpenBoa Pattern Behavior Study

This is an internal motion and composition vocabulary for the OpenBoa scale
field. It is not a collection of page-ready effects.

## Usage rule

Start from the meaning and spatial needs of a screen. Select a relevant
behavior principle, then author a new composition around that screen's text,
aperture, rhythm, and transition. Do not copy a lab state directly into a
production page.

## Invariants

- Use the official antialiased OpenBoa scale cutout.
- Preserve the landing field's scale size, density, overlap, and terracotta to
  off-white material continuity.
- Keep each scale opaque. Use ordered overlap and color transition instead of
  transparent upper scales revealing lower scales.
- Let many small units create a larger collective behavior.
- Prefer continuous propagation, coordination, and reform over unrelated
  shimmer or decorative particle motion.
- Treat aperture as one possible material state, not a mandatory text-shaped
  hole.

## Visibility

The interactive `/pattern` route is available only through its direct URL. It
is not linked from the site navigation, is excluded from the public sitemap,
and requests `noindex, nofollow`. The study source remains available for future
page-specific composition work.
